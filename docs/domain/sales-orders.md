# Sales orders and allocation

Sales owns the outbound commercial reason for stock: a compact Customer master, Sales Order and lines, and immutable links from a line to Inventory reservations. Inventory remains authoritative for stock dimensions and reserved quantity; Catalog owns variant identity and base UOM; Warehouse owns fulfillment topology.

Customers use tenant-unique `CUS-NNNNNN` numbers generated by a locked counter. Their lifecycle is `DRAFT → ACTIVE ↔ INACTIVE`, with `ACTIVE/INACTIVE → BLOCKED` and draft/inactive/blocked archival. Only an active customer can have a draft order confirmed. CRM, credit control, contracts and customer pricing are outside V1.

Sales Orders use tenant/year-scoped `SO-YYYY-NNNNNN` counters. A draft can change its destination, requested date, active fulfillment warehouse, and lines. Confirmation requires at least one line plus an active customer, warehouse and variants; it freezes commercial lines. The commercial lifecycle includes `DRAFT → CONFIRMED → PARTIALLY_ALLOCATED → ALLOCATED`, with cancellation allowed before picking. Picking, packing and dispatch advance separate fulfillment/shipping projections through order completion.

Each line snapshots variant UUID, SKU, description, canonical base UOM, explicit unit price and ISO 4217 currency. Quantities and tax-exclusive subtotals use exact `NUMERIC(19,6)`/`BigDecimal`; the server derives every subtotal. No discounts or tax engine are implied. Shipping and optional billing destinations, customer identity and warehouse identity are also snapshots.

Allocation is explicit and synchronous. Sales requests deterministic eligible candidates from Inventory within the order's single fulfillment warehouse. Lot stock is FEFO (null expiry last); untracked locations and serials use stable topology/identity ordering. Each successful claim is an Inventory reservation with `referenceType=SALES_ORDER_LINE`, no expiry, and an immutable SalesAllocation link. Inventory advisory locks and balance constraints prevent over-allocation. A shortfall is accepted: `backordered = ordered - allocated`, and the order becomes `PARTIALLY_ALLOCATED`. A later explicit allocate call retries only the remainder.

Cancellation atomically releases every active linked reservation, marks SalesAllocation history released, and clears the line allocation projection. Confirmation, allocation, reallocation and cancellation never write an InventoryMovement or InventoryLedgerEntry: on-hand is unchanged while reserved and ATP change. Reconciliation compares each line projection with active linked Inventory reservations and fails closed on drift.

Tenant-scoped foreign keys, tenant-filtered access, membership authorization, optimistic versions, bounded pages, allow-listed sorting, audit records and internal events protect the boundary. Picking, packing and shipping are implemented in Fulfillment and documented separately. V1 intentionally excludes quotations, carrier integrations, invoicing, payments, VAT, returns, promotions, advanced pricing, credit limits and commerce integrations.
